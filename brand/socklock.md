# SockLock — brand facts for ad copy

> This file is inserted into the prompt as-is. Edit it when the offer or rules change.
> Everything here is the single source of truth: the model must not state facts that are not in this file.

## Product
- Magnetic socks that don't get lost in the washing machine.
- A small magnet on the cuffs holds the pair together: take them off, click the cuffs together, wash and dry them as one pair.

## Offer (only these terms may appear in ads)
- Subscription: first box is 5 pairs for $29.
- After that, a new delivery every quarter (every 3 months).
- Do not mention discounts, free shipping, free trials, cancellation terms, or guarantees — they are not part of the offer.

## Funnel
- Every ad sends people to a short personalisation quiz (6 steps: style, size, etc.), then a personal offer.
- CTAs should invite people to take the quiz, not to "buy now".

## Market and voice
- Main market: USA. Write US English, conversational, the way people talk on Reels/TikTok.
- Tone: friendly, witty, relatable everyday-laundry moments. No shaming.

## Hard rules (ad policy and legal)
- No health, medical or therapeutic claims about magnets (circulation, pain relief, energy, etc.).
- No safety claims ("safe for everyone").
- No invented statistics, customer counts, ratings, reviews, awards or names. If a proof element is needed, use a placeholder in square brackets, e.g. [REAL REVIEW QUOTE], [REAL CUSTOMER COUNT].
- No absolute promises: "never lose a sock again", "100%", "guaranteed", "forever".
- No fake urgency or scarcity ("today only", "selling out").
- Do not name or show competitors.
